About

Founded by drivers, run like a fleet

We started with three scooters in Thamel in 2016. Today we're one of Nepal's most trusted rental fleets.

Our story

Built from breakdowns and good maps

Our founders met fixing a rented bike on the Ring Road. Every policy we have — transparent pricing, insured fleets, real roadside help — comes from what travellers told us went wrong elsewhere.

  • Founded in Kathmandu, 2016
  • 400+ vehicle fleet across 30+ districts
  • In-house mechanics service every return
  • 24/7 assistance for every active rental

How we run

The fleet standard

  1. Service after every rental
