Rider tips

Before you ride

Do you provide helmets?

Two helmets, a lock and a phone mount come with every rental at no extra cost.

Can I ride to Mustang or Manang?

Yes — Himalayan-class bikes only, with a route clearance we arrange for you.

What if I drop the bike?

Minor cosmetic damage is covered under the standard insurance; report any incident within 24 hours.
