Good to know

Rental questions

What do I need for self-drive?

A valid driving licence (Nepali, or international permit for visitors) and a refundable deposit. You must be 21+.

Is fuel included?

Self-drive: no — full-to-full policy. With-driver: fuel for the agreed route is included.

Can I cross district borders?

Yes. Mountain and restricted zones need a route clearance which we arrange for you.

What happens if the vehicle breaks down?

Call the 24/7 line — we send roadside help or a replacement vehicle, typically within 2-4 hours.

Do you rent to international visitors?

Yes, and we're the most common choice for RE Himalayan riders. We'll walk you through permit and licence rules.
